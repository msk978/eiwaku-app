**1. Higher Education vs. Vocational Training**

Some might claim that pursuing higher education is the most effective path for young people because it expands their future choices. A good example of this can be observed in Japan, where the majority of high school students choose to enter universities to secure stable jobs in large corporations. Additionally, higher education institutions provide a suitable environment for individuals to develop advanced knowledge and critical thinking skills. Therefore, earning a university degree plays a pivotal role in achieving long-term career success.

However, others assert that vocational training is more beneficial for young individuals. Firstly, it focuses heavily on practical skills that directly align with current industry demands. For instance, tech school students can learn coding and get IT jobs immediately. Secondly, this pathway helps students save money and avoid substantial student debt. As can be seen in vocational school statistics, shorter courses enable young people to enter the workforce sooner, which reduces their financial burden.
